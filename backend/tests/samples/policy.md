# Acme Corp Travel and Expense Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how employees of Acme Corp may incur and claim business travel expenses. It applies to all full-time and part-time staff.

## 2. Reimbursement Limits
Employees may claim up to 500 USD per trip for meals and incidentals without pre-approval. Hotel stays are capped at 220 USD per night in Tier 1 cities and 150 USD per night elsewhere. Any amount above these limits requires written approval from a director before the trip.

| Expense type | Daily limit (USD) | Approval needed above limit |
|---|---|---|
| Meals | 75 | Manager |
| Hotel Tier 1 | 220 | Director |
| Hotel other | 150 | Director |
| Ground transport | 60 | Manager |

## 3. Submission Deadlines
Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the trip end date through the expense portal. Late claims are paid at the discretion of Finance and may be rejected after 90 days.

## 4. Non-reimbursable Items
Alcohol, personal entertainment, fines, and upgrades to business class on flights under six hours are not reimbursable. Spouse travel is never reimbursable.
